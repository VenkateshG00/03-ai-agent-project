## Step 3 — Key Research Findings

### LinkedIn Data Points

- People who post consistently on LinkedIn get up to **5× more profile views** and **10× more connection requests** than those who don't post
- Posts with images generate **2× higher engagement** than text-only content
- The optimal cadence for building a brand quickly is **3× per week minimum** (not daily — quality matters more than frequency at low volume)
- A keyword-optimized LinkedIn profile is one of the fastest ways to be discovered by hiring managers — use terms like "RAG", "LangChain", "Prompt Engineering", "AI Automation" in your headline and About section
- Aim for at least a **150-word About section** that answers: who you are, what you're building, and why it matters

### Content Strategy

The 40/30/20/10 content ratio that works for AI professionals:
- **40% Educational** — teach something you know: "What is RAG?", "5 AI tools every startup should know"
- **30% Opinion/Perspective** — your take on trends: "Why I chose N8N over Zapier", "The AI skill nobody talks about"
- **20% Personal Experience** — build in public: "I built X in 5 days. Here's what broke and how I fixed it"
- **10% Direct Value** — things people can save and use: prompt templates, GitHub links, tool checklists, frameworks

### GitHub Portfolio Research

Recruiters and engineering managers look for **proof of technical competence** — not credentials. For AI developers in India specifically:

- Static notebooks are the weakest signal. **Live deployments beat screenshots 10×.**
- Every pinned repo needs: problem statement → architecture → live URL → tech stack → learnings and metrics
- A GitHub profile README with photo, bio, tech stack badges, and "currently building" section adds significant credibility
- For RAG projects: include recall@10, RAGAS scores, or any eval metric. No metric = "toy project" signal to hiring managers
- A **firstnamelastname.ai domain** hosting a simple portfolio page (GitHub Pages is free) adds 10× credibility vs a raw GitHub URL
- Recruiters decide whether to review your portfolio in under 10 seconds — your pinned repos are your first impression

### Networking in Hyderabad

- AGI House Hyderabad: 380+ AI enthusiasts, active meetup community
- T-Hub 2.0: world's largest startup incubator — events, workshops, community Slack
- HYSEA forums: 300+ member companies, regular industry events
- CIE IIIT-H: connects students and professionals with AI startups
- Strategy: comment thoughtfully before connecting — add a data point or example, not "great post". Then send a personalized DM with specific context.

## Step 4 — Analysis

### Pattern 1: "Build in Public" Outperforms Credentials Every Time
In AI hiring — especially at Hyderabad startups — a LinkedIn post saying "I shipped this RAG chatbot to 50 users and here's what the recall@10 looked like" beats an IBM certificate screenshot every single time. Employers are looking for builders, not learners.

### Pattern 2: The Specific Beats the Generic
"AI enthusiast" is invisible. "Building LLM-powered tools for Indian EdTech" is a category. The more specific your niche, the faster you build an audience and attract the right employers.

### Pattern 3: Consistency Over Intensity
30 posts in 30 days beats 10 posts in week 1 then silence. LinkedIn's algorithm rewards consistent engagement. Build a 7-day content calendar in advance and treat posting like a daily discipline for the month.

### Pattern 4: Your First Project Doesn't Need to Be Perfect
It needs to be deployed and documented. A live Vercel URL with a detailed GitHub README demonstrating what you built and what you learned signals more maturity than a polished but undeployed "final project."

## Step 5 — Complete Structured Report

### LinkedIn Strategy

**Profile optimization (Day 1–2):**

1. **Headline:** "AI Tools Specialist | Building LLM apps for [domain] | Hyderabad" — never "fresher" or "student"
2. **About section (150 words):** Who you are → what you're currently building → why it matters → what you're looking for. Use keywords: RAG, LangChain, Prompt Engineering, N8N, Agentic AI
3. **Featured section:** Link to your 3 GitHub projects and deployed URLs — this is the first thing recruiters check after your headline
4. **Skills section:** Add: Prompt Engineering, Python, LangChain, RAG, LangGraph, OpenAI API, Supabase, React, N8N/Make.com, Generative AI
5. **Banner image:** A simple, clean image with your niche statement — Canva AI can generate this in 5 minutes

**Content calendar (ongoing):**
- Mon: Educational post (how-to, explainer, tool comparison)
- Wed: Opinion/perspective (your take on an AI trend or tool decision)
- Fri: Build-in-public or personal story (what you built, what you learned)

### Networking Approach

**Week 1 — Map the ecosystem:**
- Follow 20 Hyderabad AI leaders on LinkedIn: T-Hub founders, AGI House members, Darwinbox AI team members, IIIT-H researchers
- Join AGI House Hyderabad community (agihouse.in)
- Sign up for T-Hub newsletter and event alerts

**Week 2 — Engage before connecting:**
- Comment on 5 posts per week from your target connections — add a data point, challenge an assumption, or share a relevant example. Never "Great post!"
- After commenting 2–3 times, send a personalized connection request: "Hi [Name], I've been following your work on [specific topic]. I'm building [X] and would love to connect."

**Week 3 — Build relationships:**
- Send 5 DMs to people who engaged with your posts — share context on your background, ask 1 specific question about their work
- Attend 1 offline or virtual AI event in Hyderabad — introduce yourself as "building AI tools" not as "looking for a job"

**Week 4 — Convert:**
- Ask 2 connections for warm introductions to hiring managers at target companies
- Share your portfolio with the AGI House community for feedback — this drives real profile views from the right people
- Apply to 5 target roles with your GitHub URL prominently in the application

### Recommendations

**1. Specificity is your unfair advantage**
"AI enthusiast" is noise. "Building RAG pipelines for Indian EdTech" is a signal. Every piece of content, every project, every LinkedIn post should reinforce one specific niche. In 30 days, you can't be known for everything — pick one problem domain and be the person who visibly solves it with AI.

**2. Deploy before you're ready**
The biggest mistake zero-experience builders make is waiting until the project is "polished" before sharing. Deploy at 80% done, share the live link, document what's still broken. "Here's what I'm building and what broke" gets 3× more engagement than "here's my finished project" — and signals exactly the kind of honesty technical hiring managers look for.

**3. Turn your course into content**
Your 30-day "Build with Claude" course is an extraordinary content asset that most people don't have. Day 14's quiz platform with Supabase auth, cohort analytics, and Recharts visualizations? That's a LinkedIn post. The PWA-to-offline-support journey? That's a post. Every day of your course is a story. Write it.
