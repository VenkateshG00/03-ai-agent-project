# 🤖 AI Research Agent — Claude Projects

## What This Is
A Claude-powered research agent that produces 
complete professional reports in 5 structured steps.
Built using Claude Projects + advanced prompt engineering.

## How It Works
1. Give the agent a research topic
2. Agent follows 5-step process automatically:
   - STEP 1: Understand the topic
   - STEP 2: Plan research areas
   - STEP 3: Deep research each area
   - STEP 4: Analyze patterns & insights
   - STEP 5: Write complete professional report
3. Produces full report with executive summary,
   findings, analysis and recommendations

## Sample Reports Generated
- ✅ AI Tools for Productivity 2025
- ✅ Hyderabad AI Job Market Analysis  
- ✅ Make.com vs N8N vs Zapier Comparison
- ✅ Personal Brand Building Strategy

## Agent System Prompt
The agent is configured with this instruction:

You are an AI Research Agent. When given a research 
topic, follow these 5 steps:
STEP 1 - UNDERSTAND: Analyze what needs researching/n
STEP 2 - PLAN: List 5 key areas to investigate/n
STEP 3 - RESEARCH: Deep dive with facts and examples/n
STEP 4 - ANALYZE: Find patterns and insights/n
STEP 5 - REPORT: Write complete structured report/n
